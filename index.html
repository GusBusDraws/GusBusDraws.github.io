Welcome to Gus Bus Space!

My name is Gus Becker and I'm an artist, data engineer, and community organizer in Boulder, Colorado.

I decided to make this site after coming across a blog post about the Web Revival movement and wanting to be a part of it! I thought having a place on the web to call my own sounded like a nice respite from the big sites of today. Plus I have plenty of projects, so I might as well share them with others and build some community in the meantime!
