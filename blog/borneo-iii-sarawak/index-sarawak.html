Sarawak, Malaysia

Sarawak is the state that makes up

Tue Jun 17 - Arriving in Kuching

Before Kuching, we have to make a stop in the Sabah city of Kota Kinabalu (KK). We booked our flight through our credit card rewards system, which means even though the flight from Tawau to KK and the flight from KK to Kuching are both operated by AirAsia, they were booked as individual flights and we have to retrieve our checked bags and re-check them before getting on the second flight. We didn't think this would be a problem, but we also didn't know that each state of Malaysia has some leftover laws from before they were unified as Malaysia that now manifest as having separate customs. This means that somehow ended up at the wrong immigration desk, and had to do some weird back-tracking through the airport before we even found our bags. Once we finally found them pulled off to the side at an office (everyone else had already retrieved the other bags), we then had to go back up to the check-in area. While we were doing the drop-off using some automated system, one of us must have leaned some extra weight onto the first bag, because by the time we tried the second one, we were over the weight limit (even though we were under this morning). The first bag had already left, but they were able to flag it so we could try to weigh it again, but that meant back-tracking through the airport AGAIN and picking up the bag from the baggage area AGAIN before we could have a second shot. Luckily, once we retrieved the bag and put it back through, the weights were fine and we could actually go through security again. The robot capitalists strike again!

After the whole bag debacle, we still make it to our gate before boarding starts. Our second flight was actually delayed by like an hour, and I'm not sure if we would have made it otherwise. So that's at least something to be grateful for. The flight goes fine, and we land in Kuching around 4pm (this time knowing to be careful we follow the signs to the right immigration area) and take a Grab to The Borneo Hotel where we're staying. This is one of the nicest hotels we've stayed in so far, figuring it would feel good to stay in a nice place after getting so dirty in the jungle. We booked a couple nights here for RM170 a night (40 USD), figuring some of the extra cost could be justified by an included breakfast. It's a clean space, seems like a good location, and the staff is very nice.

After we get settled, we walk to a place next door called Indah Cafe, which has some good reviews on the GF app. We get some nasi lemak with fried chicken, which Kaila gets to try for the first time since they fry their chicken in rice flour here. There's a lot going on in this space, and it seems like they have a lot of collaboration with local artists, so we're happy to support them. We even chat with one of the employees who tells us about a "digital nomad" info session that they're osting in a couple days. This isn't exactly our scene, but it is interesting to hear that Malaysia is starting some kind of a year long visa for remote-working foreigners. I'm not too sure of how this would work, but I make note to look into this more because I would love to have the opportunity to live closer to the rainforest and figure out how I could help with some of the conservation work going on here. Anyway, once we finish eating, we head back to the hotel, get ready for bed, and take it easy for the rest of the night with some reading and working on the blog.

Wed Jun 18 - A rest day

By design, our time in Kuching is supposed to be a bit of a break. We have been moving around a lot and not sleeping much, especially with going to bed late after night walks and getting up early for morning walks. Today we get up with just enough time to go downstairs for the included breakfast. It's pretty good, with stir fried noodles, fried rice, chicken sausages, made-to-order omelets, and watermelon. Surprisingly, the highlight for me is a toast station that has a tasty coconut jam (for some reason the jam looks more like it should taste like apricots).

After breakfast, we go back up to the room and I have some blog time while Kaila catches up with a friend. Then we go down the street to do some laundry. The time in the jungle really did a number on our option for clothes that are not too dirty to wear. Some more time hanging in the room after that.
